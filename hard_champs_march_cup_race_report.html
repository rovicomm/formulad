Race Summary

The Hard Champs March Cup opened under a sodden sky — rain confirmed before a single wheel had turned in anger. Every qualifying lap was conducted in treacherous conditions, tyres skating wide across the circuit, and the field carried the scars of those penalties into the race itself.

Formula None Car #2 set the pole lap — the fastest and cleanest run of the session, a full two rounds quicker than most of the field. Vanguard Racing was consistently competitive across all three cars, though Cars #1 and #3 each lost time at the corners. Kinetik Motorsport had a difficult hour: Car #1 ran wide twice and finished at the back of the grid. Formula None Car #1 had an equally costly session, going deep at La Ferme and conceding significant time.

The rain never let up through the opening laps. The field ran in close quarters on a circuit offering no margin for error, and the attrition began almost immediately. Formula None Car #1 arrived at a braking zone already under pressure, made contact with two Vanguard Racing cars in quick succession, and on the second hit there was simply nothing left. The car was beached in the barriers. Out before the end of Lap 1.

Kinetik Motorsport Car #1 had been carrying damage since the opening sequence. Caught out by standing water through a fast section, the car snapped sideways and collected its own teammate — Car #3 survived, but Car #1 did not. Retired mid-lap. With the field thinning, Formula None Car #3 used a nitro surge to move past Vanguard Racing Car #3 through a fast sequence — one of the cleaner overtakes of the afternoon, and the last meaningful move the car would make. It was destroyed shortly after in a heavy collision with Kinetik Motorsport Car #3 in a crowded braking zone. Kinetik Motorsport Car #3 took a hit too, but kept going.

With four cars retired and the rain refusing to ease, both surviving championship contenders read the race correctly. Formula None Car #2 and Kinetik Motorsport Car #2 emerged from their pit windows neck and neck, both with fresh rubber and full fuel loads. What followed was a lap of sustained pressure, both drivers reaching for the nitro boost repeatedly. Formula None Car #2 fired first, holding track position through a key section. Kinetik Motorsport Car #2 responded in kind, keeping the gap honest. Vanguard Racing played their hand more desperately — Cars #1, #2, and #3 all deploying nitro within the same lap, Car #3 going particularly deep into a corner and paying for it with damaged tyres.

Vanguard Racing Car #1 had been fighting the circuit throughout the second lap — repeated oversteer moments, contact with a teammate, tyres and brakes progressively giving way. The end came at a fast right-hander: the car went in too hot, stepped out in the wet, and flew off the road by two car lengths. Four retirements had now reduced the field to five runners.

The closing stages settled the order without drama. Formula None Car #2 crossed the line first — measured, clean, never in serious danger. Kinetik Motorsport Car #2 followed in second, the pit stop call having been the decisive factor. Kinetik Motorsport Car #3, battered but upright, came home third. Vanguard Racing salvaged what they could: Car #2 in fourth, and Car #3 — which had spun out twice and somehow kept going — crossed the line fifth.

On aggregate team scoring, Kinetik Motorsport's three finishers combined for 1,330 points — the outright team win. Formula None took race victory with Car #2 but their two retirements limited the team to 1,021 points. Vanguard Racing, fastest in qualifying and suffering four retirements, collected 903 points from the wreckage of their afternoon.

Verdicts
Driver of the Race
Formula None Car #2
Pole to flag. Fast when it mattered, disciplined when others weren't. Perfect wet weather management from start to finish.
Team of the Race
Kinetik Motorsport
One retirement, three points finishes. Smart pit call, smart race. Led the championship standings from the first round.
Hard Luck
Vanguard Racing
Fastest in qualifying. Four retirements across the team. The rain found every weakness and the nitro gamble backfired badly.
Qualifying Notes
Formula None — Qualifying Overview
Car #2 set the pole lap — the fastest and cleanest run of the session. Car #1 had a costly session, going deep at La Ferme and conceding significant time. Rain compounded every exit across all three entries.
Kinetik Motorsport — Qualifying Overview
Car #1 ran wide twice and finished at the back of the grid. Car #2 and Car #3 were serviceable but unremarkable in the wet. The team started from a deficit and made up for it in the race.
Vanguard Racing — Qualifying Overview
Consistently competitive across all three cars — the strongest qualifying team on the day. Cars #1 and #3 each lost time at the corners, but the pace was evident. The wet conditions that followed undid all of it.
