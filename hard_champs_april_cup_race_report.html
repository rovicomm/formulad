Race Summary

The Hard Champs March Cup opened under a sodden sky, the weather die delivering rain before a single wheel had turned in anger. Every qualifying lap was conducted in treacherous conditions — tyres skating wide at The Harbour, Superlapin Island and the Pizzeria chicane — and the field carried the scars of those penalties into the race itself.

Vanguard Racing Car #3 produced the sharpest qualifying performance of the session, completing their lap in just 27 rounds without a single corner overshoot, earning a front-row grid slot. Formula None, by contrast, suffered brutally across all three cars — Car #3 alone collected eight corner penalties over 23 rounds, setting the tone for what would prove a punishing weekend for the team's machinery.

Race day began as qualifying had ended: in the wet. The changeable weather declaration meant conditions could shift at any moment, and so they did. Cars snarled through the opening rounds in close quarters — Vanguard Racing' two entries colliding with each other as early as Round 4, costing Car #2 bodywork. Formula None answered with an early nitro injection on Car #1 through Round 3, attempting to seize track position before the field had settled.

The attrition began in earnest at Round 7. With the rain still falling and the field bunched through Superlapin Island, Kinetik Motorsport Car #2 pushed twelve spaces beyond the corner limit and was immediately black-flagged — the car's destruction leaving Kinetik Motorsport to fight on with just two runners. Moments later, Vanguard Racing Car #3 was eliminated in the same sequence, missing two mandatory stops at a single corner and suffering the same instantaneous retirement. Two cars in one round: the race had drawn its first blood.

Midway through the event the conditions relented. Formula None Car #3, running at full pace in Round 14, triggered a weather roll — and the clouds cleared. The field transitioned from wet to Good conditions, with Kinetik Motorsport immediately exploiting the dry surface to push Car #1 to maximum velocity, surviving a motor damage check in the process.

The respite was brief for Formula None Car #2. By Round 32, engine wear had accumulated beyond any margin. A high-speed pass through an open section triggered a mandatory motor damage check — and the engine expired on the spot, sending Car #2 into retirement and reducing Formula None to a single active entry for the final phase of the race.

Round 34 delivered the most dramatic moment of the afternoon. Formula None Car #1 and Vanguard Racing Car #1 — both critically damaged — collided head-on in traffic. Both cars' bodywork failed simultaneously in the same impact, both crews summoning marshals, and both machines were dragged from the circuit. Four retirements in total had now redrawn the competitive order entirely.

The closing rounds settled into a straightforward duel. Vanguard Racing Car #2 reached the line first in Round 42, banking the maximum points haul for the team. Kinetik Motorsport Car #1, aided by a late nitro surge through Round 43 that stretched the gap to Formula None, crossed in second two rounds later. Formula None Car #3 — the last survivor of a team ravaged by the race — finished third, with Kinetik Motorsport Car #3 completing the classified order in fourth.

On aggregate team scoring, Kinetik Motorsport's two finishers combined for 1,209 points — sufficient for the outright team win despite losing Car #2 inside the opening laps. Vanguard Racing converted a race victory and 1,023 points. Formula None, starting with three cars and ending with one, scraped together 622 points from the wreckage of their afternoon.

Verdicts
Driver of the Race
Vanguard Racing Car #2
The cleanest qualifying run, the only nitro-assisted push into a winning position, and first across the line. A measured performance in chaotic conditions.
Team of the Race
Kinetik Motorsport
Lost a car in Round 7 and still won the team competition. Two finishers from two survivors — a composed response to early adversity.
Hard Luck
Formula None
Three cars entered. Engine failure, a double-DNF collision, and relentless rain damage throughout qualifying reduced a full team to a single points scorer.
Qualifying Notes
Formula None — Qualifying Overview
Car #1 completed 21 rounds with 3 corner overshoots. Car #2 completed 22 rounds with 4 corner overshoots. Car #3 — the worst session of the day — completed 23 rounds with 8 corner overshoots. Rain compounded every exit across all three entries.
Kinetik Motorsport — Qualifying Overview
Car #1 completed 28 rounds with 4 corner overshoots including a 3-space violation at South Turn. Car #2 completed 31 rounds with 7 overshoots, including heavy penalties at Golden Gate and The Shock Corridor. Car #3's session is not fully logged but showed similar rain-affected difficulty.
Vanguard Racing — Qualifying Overview
Car #2 produced the session's standout performance: 27 rounds, zero corner overshoots. Car #3 also completed 27 rounds with only 1 overshoot at Lost Church. Car #1 qualifying data not fully recovered in logs.
